Fintech A/B Testing & Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end A/B experiment on 50,000 simulated Nigerian fintech transactions (Jan–Dec 2024). It tests whether an email and mobile nudge improves completion among web users who abandoned a transaction, and presents the analysis in an interactive Plotly Dash app.

  • EDA across 10 customer dimensions: age, state, device, channel, tenure and more
  • Power analysis (α = 0.05, power = 0.8, effect size 0.2) to size the experiment
  • 10,918 abandoned web users split 50/50 into control and treatment
  • Chi-square test: χ² = 443.7, p < 0.0001, odds ratio = 2.27
  • The nudge lifted completion from 38% to 57%
  • Three-tier architecture: repository, business logic, Dash presentation
